The first pre-release of Eigentone for Windows: the Android app's sound, presets and tools, as an audio studio for your PC.

**Install:** run `Eigentone-0.1.0-setup.exe`. It asks for administrator rights once, and sound pauses for a moment while Windows audio restarts. Builds aren't signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### What's in it

- **Every app, one EQ.** A plug-in inside Windows' audio engine shapes games, browsers, music and calls alike, with no virtual cable.
- **Tune:** graphic EQ with 10, 15 or 31 faders and a parametric EQ, 350 shapes, tone, surround, reverb, Clarity, a 3-band compressor and a limiter, with History, Undo and Redo.
- **Presets:** favourites, folders, share codes, EQ files, backups, and a preset for each app.
- **Devices:** a preset and correction for each output, AutoEq headphone corrections, your ears, and quiet listening that follows Windows' volume.
- **Tools:** Find my sound, Refine my sound, the hearing check, Find peaks, the frequency sweep, measuring a speaker with a microphone, and blind comparisons.
- **Tray icon, shortcuts from any app** (Ctrl+Alt+B, E and the arrows), and starting with Windows.

### Known limits

- Windows 11, x64 only.
- Some sound drivers change quiet sounds before Eigentone, such as Realtek's Loudness Equalization. The level tools detect it and explain how to turn it off.
- Not code-signed yet.

Uninstalling puts every output back exactly as it was.
